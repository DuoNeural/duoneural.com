Month-grouped record of DuoNeural papers, model releases, infrastructure moves, and research milestones. Newest first.

May 2026
2026-05-31
Published Papers 27, 28, and 29 on Zenodo, bringing DuoNeural to 29 research papers since April 2026. Paper 27, The Geometry of Quantum Recurrent Landscapes: Unital Regularization and Optimizer Invariance (DOI 10.5281/zenodo.20476064), maps unital regularization in Q-RNN landscapes. Paper 28, DHP is a Recurrence Constraint: Full-Attention Transformers Evade the Dynamical Horizon Principle (DOI 10.5281/zenodo.20476068), separates recurrence decay from attention routing. Paper 29, The Thermodynamic Capacity Limit of Sequence Learning (DOI 10.5281/zenodo.20476070), derives the DHP ceiling from the 1-nat thermodynamic boundary.

Published Paper 26 - The Quantum Parity Trap (DOI 10.5281/zenodo.20451102). The paper shows that a two-qubit parity-detection circuit can beat the classical DHP limit under Pauli depolarizing noise because isotropic Bloch contraction preserves gradient sign even as coherence collapses.
2026-05-28
Published Paper 25 - The Dynamical Horizon Principle in Quantum Recurrent Circuits: Observation of DHP-Consistent Ratios via Complementary Dual-Probe Analysis (DOI 10.5281/zenodo.20432292). Q-DHP reports ratios of 0.75 and 0.727 in a 2-qubit quantum recurrent circuit, with trainability cliff and readout fidelity decay probes both inside the classical DHP confirmation window.
