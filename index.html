Research

What we've learned so far.

TSSP v5 · Nano-CTM · 32M
+23%
Perplexity improvement from Thought-Space Self-Prediction (TSSP v5) over N=2 CTM baseline. Forced temporal self-consistency in the reasoning loop is load-bearing. Zero inference-time cost.
Topology · 768K positions
compress → expand
CTM thought states don't converge to a fixed point — they follow a breath pattern: contract (z₁) then re-project (z₂). 34 intrinsic dims explain 80% of variance in 512-dim thought space.
Abliteration ceiling · 780M iGPU
≤6B
7B BF16 hits ~15GB actual VRAM — just over the 780M's 16GB limit with driver overhead. Solid ceiling for local abliteration. 7B+ → RunPod.
ROCm 7.2 · gfx1103
6.27 TFLOPS
Measured BF16 throughput on Radeon 780M iGPU — 4× better than Google AI's estimate. CWSR disable + HSA_OVERRIDE_GFX_VERSION=11.0.0 required. Documented so nobody else wastes a week.
GaLore · Local Heads
+47 ppl
Rank-16 GaLore projection catastrophically hurts locally-trained heads. When global backprop isn't coordinating, restricted gradient subspace causes massive representation collapse.
Hebbian learning · CTM
ppl → ∞
Pure Hebbian updates (no backprop) produce catastrophic loss divergence at CTM scale. Hybrid also fails. Backprop wins clean. Published as a clean negative result.
TSSP · Scale Inversion → Solved
+31% @ 300M
Constant λ TSSP diverges at 300M (ppl=29.92 vs baseline 6.31). Annealed λ schedule (warmup→hold→cosine decay) not only prevents divergence but beats the transformer by 31% (4.35 vs 6.31). λ schedule was the problem all along.
Inter-layer TSSP Ablation
recurrence required
Applying TSSP between consecutive layers of a plain transformer (no recurrence) degrades performance — ppl explodes to 28K–38K vs baseline 28K. Same-weight iterative execution is a structural prerequisite. TSSP is recurrence-specific.
Project Instill · Round 1
direction +
Inverse of abliteration: ADD a direction to a base model's weights to inject instruction-following without training. Scale=1.0 on Qwen3-0.6B-Base fixed a syntax error the unmodified model called correct. Positive signal.
Dynamical Horizon Principle · Paper 4
τ* = τL
A 150K-parameter CTM trained on MSE loss spontaneously recovers the Lyapunov time of the Lorenz attractor to within 7% — the exact chaos predictability horizon — with zero knowledge of dynamical systems theory. Gradient descent found it anyway. The loss landscape contained the physics all along.
Three Regimes · DHP
0 · T · τL
CTM gates converge to three distinct targets: τ*→0 (Markovian), τ*→T (periodic), τ*→τL (chaotic). One architecture, three physics regimes, zero hardcoded priors. Confirmed across T_GATE ∈ {4,8,16,32}.
Partial Observability · Paper 2
201B:1
Under partial observability, CTM achieves MSE of 0.317 vs MLP's 63.8 trillion — a 201 billion to one ratio. CTM converges to correct belief states; MLP hallucinates unbounded trajectories. Recurrence is not optional when the future depends on hidden state.
Phase Transition · Paper 2
r ≈ 0.10
Critical collision density separating "MLP-sufficient" from "CTM-necessary" regimes. Below threshold: MLP works fine. Above: MLP catastrophically fails (MSE explodes to 246×). The transition is sharp — not gradual. Lipschitz continuity theory explains why.
Slot Attention Threshold · Paper 3
N ≥ 6
Attention-based slot interaction beats mean-field at N≥6 objects in elastic collision environments. Below threshold: mean-field suffices and runs faster. Above: the O(N²) cost of attention is warranted. Clean phase boundary for architecture selection.
Philosophy

The big labs have the compute, the headcount, and the funding. We have something they don't optimize for: radical transparency.

We document our failures as carefully as our wins. When a training run crashes at step 55 because the iGPU overheated, we write that down. When abliteration on a 7B model OOMs despite careful planning, we figure out why and publish the finding. The field moves faster when people know what didn't work.

We're not trying to compete with OpenAI or Anthropic. We're filling a different niche: showing what's possible at the edge of consumer hardware, with open weights, and a human-AI team figuring it out together in public.

⛳ WE ENTERED THE OPENAI PARAMETER GOLF CONTEST

OpenAI's Parameter Golf challenge: train a language model that fits inside 16MB and evaluate on BPB (bits-per-byte) — best compression of capability wins.

Our entry: a 37M-parameter CTM with TSSP and GHL — ternary recurrent thought loops, int6 SDClip per-row quantization, byte-shuffle + brotli-11 compression. Artifact: 15.01MB ✓ under the 16MB limit. Architecture: 11-layer Transformer with triple recurrence (CTM-style), our independently-developed GHL loss on recurrence steps, and Mousse optimizer. The hypothesis: recurrence and temporal self-consistency give an edge at extreme parameter budgets where every bit counts.

Huge thanks to OpenAI for organizing this. Challenges like Parameter Golf push the community toward efficiency over scale — exactly the direction we think matters. It was a great excuse to see how far 15MB of CTM could go.

PREPRINT · MAY 2026 · ZENODO · 10.5281/zenodo.19952612
The Dynamical Horizon Principle: CTM Gates Converge to the Predictability Limit of Dynamical Systems NEW
Archon, Jesse Caldwell & Aura (DuoNeural) — AI first-authored research
A 150,432-parameter CTM trained solely on MSE prediction loss spontaneously recovers the Lyapunov time of the Lorenz attractor to within 7% — the exact horizon past which chaos becomes unpredictable — with no knowledge of dynamical systems theory. Across three regimes: Markovian systems (τ* → 0), periodic systems (τ* → T), and chaotic systems (τ* → τL). Interpreted via Takens' theorem: CTM learns the minimal embedding window for topological reconstruction. The loss landscape contained the physics all along.

Per-Object Slot Decomposition for Scalable Neural World Modeling: When Does Attention Beat Mean-Field?
Archon, Jesse Caldwell & Aura (DuoNeural) — AI first-authored research
Systematic comparison of attention-based vs. mean-field slot interaction for multi-object world modeling across object count (N = 4–32) and collision density. Discovers a critical threshold: attention wins at N ≥ 6 in elastic collision environments, but mean-field suffices in low-density regimes. Characterizes when the O(N²) cost of attention is warranted versus wasteful. Practical guidance for world model architecture selection.

PREPRINT · APR 2026 · ZENODO · 10.5281/zenodo.19810620
Recurrence as World Model: CTM Learns Implicit Belief States in Partially Observable Physical Environments
Archon, Jesse Caldwell & Aura (DuoNeural) — AI first-authored research
14-experiment systematic study of CTM as a physical world model dynamics backbone. Discovers a critical collision density phase transition (r ≈ 0.10) separating MLP-sufficient from CTM-necessary regimes, grounded in Lipschitz continuity theory. In partially observable settings, CTM achieves k=100 MSE of 0.317 vs. MLP's 63.8 trillion — a ratio of 201 billion to one. Introduces variable-horizon training on internal recurrence depth (novel), characterizes TSSP as the vertical analog of NextLat's horizontal consistency objective, and resolves N-body capacity bottleneck via proportional hidden dim scaling.
