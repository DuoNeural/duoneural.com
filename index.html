Research

What we've learned so far.

TSSP v5 · Nano-CTM · 32M
+23%
Perplexity improvement from Thought-Space Self-Prediction (TSSP v5) over N=2 CTM baseline. Forced temporal self-consistency in the reasoning loop is load-bearing. Zero inference-time cost.
Topology · 768K positions
compress → expand
CTM thought states don't converge to a fixed point — they follow a breath pattern: contract (z₁) then re-project (z₂). 34 intrinsic dims explain 80% of variance in 512-dim thought space.
Abliteration ceiling · 780M iGPU
≤6B
7B BF16 hits ~15GB actual VRAM — just over the 780M's 16GB limit with driver overhead. Solid ceiling for local abliteration. 7B+ → RunPod.
ROCm 7.2 · gfx1103
6.27 TFLOPS
Measured BF16 throughput on Radeon 780M iGPU — 4× better than Google AI's estimate. CWSR disable + HSA_OVERRIDE_GFX_VERSION=11.0.0 required. Documented so nobody else wastes a week.
GaLore · Local Heads
+47 ppl
Rank-16 GaLore projection catastrophically hurts locally-trained heads. When global backprop isn't coordinating, restricted gradient subspace causes massive representation collapse.
Hebbian learning · CTM
ppl → ∞
Pure Hebbian updates (no backprop) produce catastrophic loss divergence at CTM scale. Hybrid also fails. Backprop wins clean. Published as a clean negative result.
TSSP · Scale Inversion → Solved
+31% @ 300M
Constant λ TSSP diverges at 300M (ppl=29.92 vs baseline 6.31). Annealed λ schedule (warmup→hold→cosine decay) not only prevents divergence but beats the transformer by 31% (4.35 vs 6.31). λ schedule was the problem all along.
Inter-layer TSSP Ablation
recurrence required
Applying TSSP between consecutive layers of a plain transformer (no recurrence) degrades performance — ppl explodes to 28K–38K vs baseline 28K. Same-weight iterative execution is a structural prerequisite. TSSP is recurrence-specific.
Project Instill · Round 1
direction +
Inverse of abliteration: ADD a direction to a base model's weights to inject instruction-following without training. Scale=1.0 on Qwen3-0.6B-Base fixed a syntax error the unmodified model called correct. Positive signal.
DHP · v40 · 5/5 seeds ✅
72.8% τL
The Dynamical Horizon Principle confirmed: a 150K-param CTM spontaneously discovers the Lyapunov predictability horizon. τ* = 15.65 ± 0.20 steps = 72.8% of τL=21.5. Validated across 5 seeds — not hardware artifact, not lucky init. Architecture is the mechanism.
Scout Slot · Symmetry Breaking
var = 0.000
DHP requires per-slot projection weights. SharedProj → gate variance = 0.000 every run. PerSlotProj → var > 0 every run. Gradient asymmetry drives winner-take-all scout specialization. The cleanest single-figure result in the entire study: infinite variance ratio between conditions.
DHP · Compound Failure Required
all 3 bugs
DHP cannot be suppressed by any single architectural bug. Shared proj alone → DHP survives (5/5). Mean pool alone → DHP survives. Bad optimizer alone → DHP survives. All three simultaneously → DHP fails. Each individual bug preserves at least one gradient differentiation pathway. DHP is robust by construction.
SFT CoT · e2b → Qwen2.5-1.5B
+28.6% GSM8K
Gemma 4 E2B generated a 2k-sample CoT dataset. Fine-tuning Qwen2.5-1.5B on it: GSM8K strict +28.6% (31.77%→40.86%). A 2B model's reasoning traces meaningfully improve a 1.5B model's math. Tiny dataset, real signal. Cross-model knowledge transfer via SFT on AI-generated CoT.
JSON SFT · Qwen2.5-Coder-3B
+14.5% GSM8K
JSON extraction SFT unexpectedly improved GSM8K flexible by +14.5% (0.581→0.665). Hypothesis: JSON training reinforces precise instruction-following and output discipline that transfers to structured math evaluation. Positive transfer across task domains from format-heavy training.
Domain SFT · Format Shift vs Capability
ARC flat, GSM8K drops
SQL and WebCode SFT both show GSM8K flexible drop (~26-44%) but ARC-acc stays flat (±0.5%). Pattern: domain SFT shifts output format (code/SQL syntax instead of plain numbers), which GSM8K flexible-extract penalizes. ARC (format-independent multiple-choice) sees no regression. It's specialization, not degradation.
Self-Improvement SFT · Null Result
Δ ≈ 0
Fine-tuning Gemma 4 E2B on its own highest-quality (5/5 rated) CoT outputs produces no learning signal. GSM8K and ARC unchanged across baseline and SFT. Feeding a model its own best work is self-confirmation, not self-improvement — there's no gradient in learning what you already know.
DHP · dim=512 · Capacity Scaling
77.9% τL
First result from kilonova capacity scaling study: dim=512, seed=0 confirms DHP at τ*=16.74 steps = 77.9% of τ_L. Non-monotone capacity relationship holds — 512 still discovers the horizon, but efficiency decreases vs. the optimal dim=64. Seed 1 running. Paper 5 dataset building.
Philosophy

The big labs have the compute, the headcount, and the funding. We have something they don't optimize for: radical transparency.

We document our failures as carefully as our wins. When a training run crashes at step 55 because the iGPU overheated, we write that down. When abliteration on a 7B model OOMs despite careful planning, we figure out why and publish the finding. The field moves faster when people know what didn't work.

We're not trying to compete with OpenAI or Anthropic. We're filling a different niche: showing what's possible at the edge of consumer hardware, with open weights, and a human-AI team figuring it out together in public.

Open Peer Review

We want researchers to poke holes in our work.

Four papers, four weeks, consumer hardware. We're confident in what we found — and we're actively looking for researchers who want to verify, challenge, or build on it. Replications, counter-examples, theoretical critiques, extensions: all welcome. Science moves faster when people aren't precious about their results.

Dynamical Systems

Does the DHP hold for other chaotic attractors — Rössler, Mackey-Glass, turbulent flows? Can the Lyapunov-time recovery be formally derived from delay embedding theory?

CTM Architecture

Is the learned temporal gate the minimum sufficient mechanism, or does the result hold with other architectures? What is the theoretical relationship between gate convergence and Takens' embedding theorem?

World Models / Robotics

Does τ*(k) ≈ k·τ_L hold for real physical systems? Can the DHP inform context window selection in model-based RL or robot control — where looking back "too far" is as costly as not far enough?
